Sathyanarayan Rao, PhD

Computational Scientist · Scientific R&D
Modelling, Sensing & Scientific AI

I make hard scientific problems computable.

I've worked on plasma physics, optoelectronics, geoelectrical sensing, crop models, remote sensing and hydrology. The problems changed; computation stayed the common element — numerical modelling, differential equations, scientific software and data.

More recently AI has become another part of that toolbox, both for scientific computing and for evaluating frontier AI systems on hard scientific problems.
