Sathyanarayan Rao, PhD

Computational Scientist · Scientific R&D
Modelling, Sensing & Scientific AI

I work on systems that are difficult to observe directly. Roots under a field, water held in soil seen from orbit, a plasma no probe survives, a crop that hasn't grown yet. The method has been the same for nineteen years: build a physical model, understand how the observations actually arise, and use computation to infer the processes underneath.

Lately the same habit has turned on AI systems — you can only partly see how they reason, so you design the probes and infer from what comes back.
