Nineteen years across plasma physics, optoelectronics, root geophysics, agricultural
modelling, Earth observation and scientific AI. Described by subject that looks like
six careers. Described by method it is one: **model a system you can only partly
observe, work out how its signals arise, and use computation to infer the processes
underneath.**
